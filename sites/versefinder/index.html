Simple study method

Use this four-step method with any passage you look up.

  1. Read: Read the passage slowly twice.
  2. Observe: Notice repeated words, commands, promises, and contrasts.
  3. Reflect: Ask what the passage reveals about God, people, and faithful action.
  4. Respond: Write one sentence of prayer or one concrete action for today.

Helpful reflection questions

What is the main idea of this passage? What does it invite me to trust? What does it ask me to change? Who could be encouraged by this verse today?
