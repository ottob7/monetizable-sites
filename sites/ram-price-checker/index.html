Buying signal

What matters when RAM is expensive?

  • Price per GB: the fastest way to see whether a kit is actually cheap.
  • Capacity first: 32GB is the sweet spot for gaming and creator PCs; 64GB+ matters for local AI, heavy video, and workstations.
  • Platform fit: DDR5 prices do not help if your board only accepts DDR4.
  • Latency and speed: DDR5-6000 CL30-class kits are common sweet spots for AMD/Intel desktops.
